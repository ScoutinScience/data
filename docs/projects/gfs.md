GFS scores how fundable a grant proposal is. Its data are proposals with their outcomes, collected into the [GFS research corpus]({{ "/storage/gfs-corpus.html" | relative_url }}).

## Source tiers

| Tier | Meaning |
|:--|:--|
| **A** | Two-sided: proposal text with funded **and** rejected outcomes |
| **B** | Full proposal text, funded only (or no decision) |
| **C** | Funded abstracts and metadata — base rates, no proposal text |
| **D** | Proxy: conference papers with accept/reject, not grants |

Large two-sided corpora exist but sit behind data agreements; they aren't listed here until access is granted.
